Our history

How it began

Divine Love Ministry began in 2020 in Kasoa, Central Region, as a benevolent initiative by Rev. Fr. Charles Bekpar to support the aged, the sick, and the less privileged. In those early days, the work was funded entirely through Fr. Bekpar's personal contributions and donations from family and friends.

The initiative supported small business set-ups for women, donated food items and cash to the sick and the aged, and delivered supplies to orphanages — quiet acts of love that steadily grew into something larger.

On Sunday, 13th September 2022, the organization was officially launched at Mercy Women Catholic Hospital, Mankessim. Since then, Divine Love Ministry has organized free National Health Insurance registration for four communities, free health screening and treatment, health education, sports activities for the youth, and ongoing donations to orphanages, the sick, and the aged.

Our founder

Rev. Fr. Charles Bekpar

Rev. Fr. Charles Bekpar is a Catholic priest and the Chaplain of Mercy Women Catholic Hospital, Mankessim, in the Central Region of Ghana. Moved by the daily struggles of the aged, the sick, and the less privileged around him, he began Divine Love Ministry as a personal act of service — funding its earliest work from his own resources and the generosity of family and friends.

Every act of love, however small, restores dignity to someone who needs it.

Our purpose

Mission & vision

Our mission

Empowering vulnerable populations in rural communities to achieve optimal health and well-being through comprehensive healthcare services, education, and community-driven initiatives, while promoting dignity, inclusivity, and social justice.

Our vision

A world where rural communities thrive, with equitable access to quality healthcare, education, and economic opportunities, enabling them to live healthy, fulfilling, and self-sustaining lives.

What guides us

Our core values

Compassion

Demonstrating empathy and kindness towards all, especially the vulnerable.

Inclusivity

Embracing diversity and promoting equal opportunities for every person.

Empowerment

Helping communities take control of their own health and well-being.

Integrity

Operating with transparency, accountability, and honesty in all we do.

Respect

Valuing the dignity and worth of every individual, culture, and community.

What we aim to achieve

Our objectives

  1. Offer essential health services to vulnerable populations.
  2. Educate communities on healthy practices and disease prevention.
  3. Support women's empowerment through education and skills training.
  4. Promote social and economic growth in rural communities.
  5. Offer support services to individuals and families in need.
