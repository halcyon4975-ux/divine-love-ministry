Who we are

A ministry of love, founded on service

Divine Love Ministry is a non-profit organization founded by Rev. Fr. Charles Bekpar, Catholic priest and Chaplain of Mercy Women Catholic Hospital, Mankessim. What began in 2020 as one priest's personal outreach to the aged, the sick, and the less privileged in Kasoa has grown into an organized movement of volunteers, donors, and well-wishers serving communities across the Central Region.

Our purpose

Mission & vision

Our mission

Empowering vulnerable populations in rural communities to achieve optimal health and well-being through comprehensive healthcare services, education, and community-driven initiatives, while promoting dignity, inclusivity, and social justice.

Our vision

A world where rural communities thrive, with equitable access to quality healthcare, education, and economic opportunities, enabling them to live healthy, fulfilling, and self-sustaining lives.
