Our programs

What we do, and why it matters

Seven areas of work, each shaped by the real needs of rural communities in Ghana's Central Region.

Healthcare outreach

Our flagship program brings free health screening, treatment, and health education directly to underserved communities — including the people of Anomabo in the Mfantseman Municipality. We also run free National Health Insurance (NHIS) registration drives, such as the December 2022 exercise that registered the people of Eduafo, Jedu, and Abura Nkwanta, giving families lasting access to care.

Free screening Treatment NHIS registration Health education

Women's empowerment

From its earliest days, the ministry has supported women in setting up small businesses, alongside education and skills training. When a woman earns a stable income, an entire household eats better, stays healthier, and keeps its children in school — which is why women's empowerment sits at the heart of our work.

Small business support Skills training Education

Community development

We believe strong communities are built together. In collaboration with St. Joseph Catholic Church, Abura Nkwanta, the ministry has organized end-of-year get-togethers that bring residents together in celebration and fellowship — alongside broader outreach that promotes social and economic growth in rural towns.

Community events Church partnerships Outreach

Youth development

Sport gives young people discipline, teamwork, and joy. The ministry organizes soccer for the young people of Abura Nkwanta and its surrounding villages, creating healthy outlets and building confidence in the next generation of community leaders.

Youth soccer Teamwork Mentorship

Elderly & sick support

Some of the ministry's most personal work happens quietly: donations of cash, food, and essential items to elderly men and women and to those battling illness. These gestures meet urgent needs — and remind each person that they are seen, valued, and loved.

Food and items Cash support Home Visits

Orphanage support

Since 2020, the ministry has made regular donations of food, clothing, and essential supplies to orphanages — ensuring that children without family support are still surrounded by care, provision, and hope for the future.
