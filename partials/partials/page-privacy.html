Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

How we handle the personal information you share with Divine Love Ministry.

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Introduction

Divine Love Ministry ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us directly:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, subject, and message.
  • Volunteer registration form: your name, email, phone number, location, occupation, skills, and your reason for volunteering.

We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts on this site, and we do not collect any information automatically beyond what you submit yourself.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide only to respond to your inquiry or to coordinate your volunteer registration — for example, contacting you to confirm your availability and match you with an outreach activity. We do not use your information for marketing, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Third parties involved in handling your information

  • Formspree processes form submissions on our behalf and delivers them to our email inbox. They receive the information you submit in order to perform this service. See Formspree's own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts loads the typefaces used on this site directly from Google's servers, which may log your IP address as part of that request. No personal form data is involved in this.

Data retention

We keep submitted messages and volunteer registrations only as long as needed to respond to you or coordinate your involvement, and we do not retain them indefinitely.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information you've given us. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
