Your generosity becomes care

Every cedi given translates into health screenings, NHIS registrations, food for the elderly, and hope for children in orphanages.

Why donate

Small gifts, real change

Divine Love Ministry is funded by personal donations from our founder, contributions from family and friends, volunteer support, and gifts from benefactors, church groups, societies, and well-wishers. There are no big grants behind this work — only people like you. That means your donation isn't a drop in an ocean; it is the ocean.

Health screening

Funds medicines, screening supplies, and treatment for community health outreaches.

NHIS registration

Covers registration fees so whole communities gain lasting access to healthcare.

Food & essentials

Provides food items and supplies for the sick, the aged, and orphanages.

Women's businesses

Seeds small business set-ups that lift women — and their families — out of poverty.

How to give

Give directly, right now

Choose whichever is easiest for you — Mobile Money or a direct bank transfer. Both reach the ministry the same way.

Mobile Money

Send from any network's Mobile Money service.

Mobile Money number 024 997 8365
Registered name Divine Love Ministry
  1. Dial your network's Mobile Money code (e.g. *170# for MTN).
  2. Choose "Send Money" / "Transfer."
  3. Enter the number above and your donation amount.
  4. Confirm the name shows as "Divine Love Ministry" before you send.
  5. Keep your confirmation SMS as your receipt.

Bank Transfer

Transfer directly to our GCB account.

Bank GCB Bank
Account name Charles Bekpar
Account number 1341010040665
Branch Kasoa Main
  1. Visit any GCB branch, or use your bank's transfer app.
  2. Select "Transfer to another account."
  3. Enter the account number above.
  4. Confirm the account name matches "Charles Bekpar."
  5. Complete your transfer and keep your teller slip or receipt.

Always double-check the recipient name before you send, and keep your confirmation message or receipt. If anything looks off, contact us before completing the transfer.
