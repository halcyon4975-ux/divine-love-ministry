Who we are

A ministry of love, founded on service

Divine Love Ministry is a non-profit organization founded by Rev. Fr. Charles Bekpar, Catholic priest and Chaplain of Mercy Women Catholic Hospital, Mankessim. What began in 2020 as one priest's personal outreach to the aged, the sick, and the less privileged in Kasoa has grown into an organized movement of volunteers, donors, and well-wishers serving communities across the Central Region.

Our purpose

Mission & vision

Our mission

Empowering vulnerable populations in rural communities to achieve optimal health and well-being through comprehensive healthcare services, education, and community-driven initiatives, while promoting dignity, inclusivity, and social justice.

Our vision

A world where rural communities thrive, with equitable access to quality healthcare, education, and economic opportunities, enabling them to live healthy, fulfilling, and self-sustaining lives.

What we do

Our programs

From free health screening in Anomabo to youth soccer in Abura Nkwanta, every program answers a real need in the communities we serve.

Healthcare outreach

Free health screening, treatment, health education, and National Health Insurance registration for underserved communities.

Women's empowerment

Seed support for small businesses, skills training, and education that help women build independent, sustainable livelihoods.

Why support us

Your support goes where it's needed most

Direct, hands-on impact

We work face-to-face in the communities we serve. Donations become health screenings, NHIS registrations, food, and care — not overhead.

Integrity and accountability

Founded and led by Rev. Fr. Charles Bekpar, the ministry operates with transparency, honesty, and accountability at every step.

Rooted in community

Programs are shaped with local leaders, churches, and residents — so every initiative answers a need the community itself has named.

Healthcare where there is none

Rural families often have no realistic access to care. Our outreach brings screening, treatment, and health insurance to their doorstep.
